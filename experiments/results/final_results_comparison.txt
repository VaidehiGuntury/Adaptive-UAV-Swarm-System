7. FINAL CONCLUSIONS
====================

Q1. How does our static implementation compare with the original paper?
    Our 2D corrected BSA+IDE implementation achieves 87.11 +/- 4.41% coverage
    at 350 s under static obstacles. The paper reports 112.3 +/- 10.6 s mission
    completion in 3D under different dimensionality, arena, physics, and stopping
    criterion. No direct numerical comparison is valid. What can be said: the
    algorithm family (BSA + IDE) is correctly implemented per the paper description,
    and the exploration behaviour is qualitatively consistent with the paper.

Q2. How does dynamic obstacle motion affect coverage relative to static?
    For 10 UAVs: slow (-1.75 pp), equal-speed (-4.06 pp), mixed (-3.97 pp)
    reduced coverage vs static. Fast obstacles increased coverage (+2.44 pp).
    For 20 UAVs: ALL dynamic scenarios INCREASED coverage vs static (slow +1.06 pp,
    equal +5.74 pp, fast +8.57 pp, mixed +7.04 pp). The pattern is not monotone
    with obstacle speed at 10 UAVs, and the direction reverses at 20 UAVs.
    The experiment indicates these associations, but does not establish the cause.

Q3. Which dynamic scenario performed WORST for 10 UAVs?
    Equal-speed obstacles: 56.61% (-4.06 pp / -6.69% vs static).

Q4. Which dynamic scenario performed BEST for 10 UAVs?
    Fast obstacles: 62.14% (+2.44 pp / +4.03% vs static).
    Note: 'best' here means highest coverage, not a normative quality judgment.

Q5. Does increasing from 10 to 20 UAVs improve robustness to moving obstacles?
    In these single-seed runs: yes, empirically. At 20 UAVs, all dynamic scenarios
    exceeded static coverage. At 10 UAVs, most dynamic scenarios reduced it. However,
    these are single-seed results and the difference may be partly due to stochasticity.
    The experiment indicates an association between fleet size and dynamic-obstacle
    impact, but does not establish the cause.

Q6. What is genuinely demonstrated by our experiments?
    - BSA + IDE corrected exploration achieves 87% 2D coverage in 350 s (5 seeds).
    - 5/5 seeds reached 80%; 2/5 seeds reached 85% within 350 s.
    - Dynamic obstacles produce non-trivial effects on coverage at both fleet sizes.
    - The search/tracking extension is functional and the search phase is reachable.
    - 20 UAVs achieve substantially more coverage than 10 UAVs at 120 s.

Q7. What cannot legitimately be claimed?
    - That our system outperforms the DEBS paper (incompatible evaluation conditions).
    - That the dynamic-obstacle coverage increases are statistically significant
      (single-seed runs, no replicates).
    - That our T85=278.8 s (2/5) can be compared to the paper's 112.3 s.
    - That results from 120 s runs are comparable to results from 350 s runs.

DISCREPANCY EXPLANATION: WHY 60.68% != 48.38%
==============================================
obstacle_scenario_validation static_n10 = 60.68% (coverage at end of 120 s run, seed 42)
paper_matched_benchmark mean 120 s snapshot = 48.38% (mean of 5 seeds)

Cause 1 — DIFFERENT SEEDS: obstacle validation uses seed 42 only.
  Paper-matched uses seeds 42,43,44,45,46. Seed 42 alone gave 51.2% at 120s
  in the paper-matched run, not 60.68%.

Cause 2 — DIFFERENT INITIALISATION PATH: obstacle validation uses
  src/main.build_simulation() which pre-marks spawn positions and performs
  engine wiring differently from the paper-matched runner's direct calls to
  World.from_config() + spawn_uavs() + SimulationEngine(). This shifts
  the initial explored fraction and early-step coverage.

Cause 3 — SAME metric definition confirmed: both report coverage fraction
  at t=120 s (end of 120 s run for obstacle validation; 120 s snapshot of
  350 s run for paper-matched). The metric is identical; the difference is
  real and driven by causes 1 and 2.

CONSEQUENCE: The obstacle validation static baseline (60.68%) is the correct
and only valid baseline for the static vs dynamic comparison in Section 4,
because all obstacle scenarios in that experiment share the same initialisation
path and seed. The 48.38% figure from the paper-matched benchmark is NOT an
alternative baseline for this comparison.

LIMITATIONS

1. Dynamic obstacle experiments: single-seed, 120 s. No variance estimate.
2. T85 and T90 reached by only 2/5 seeds in 350 s. Not all seeds converge.
3. 2D vs 3D: direct comparison to paper not valid.
4. 30-UAV dynamic obstacle experiments: not available in saved data.
5. Search/tracking: one run only; detection rate 62.5% limited by UAV coverage.
6. Extended single-seed result not replicated in the 5-seed benchmark.
